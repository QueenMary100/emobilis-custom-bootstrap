Delivery & Terms

Please note: This site is a course project for a fictional brand. Orders cannot be placed here in real life; the policies below show how a real roastery would present them.

Delivery

Nyeri town and Nairobi: by our own riders, usually within 48 hours of roasting, free on the Daily Drinker and Office Pot plans and KES 200 otherwise. Rest of Kenya: by courier, 2 to 4 working days, fee confirmed before you pay.

Ordering and payment

Orders are placed by WhatsApp or email. Payment is by M-Pesa; we confirm receipt before roasting your order. We roast on Mondays and Thursdays, so orders confirmed by Wednesday noon ship from Thursday's roast.

Subscriptions

Plans renew monthly. Pause, skip or cancel by message before Wednesday noon; no cancellation fees.

Freshness promise

If your coffee is not right, tell us within seven days and we will replace it or refund you. We never ship beans older than seven days from the roast date.

Privacy

We use your name, phone number or email only to process your order and answer your message. We do not sell your data. This website does not use cookies or analytics of its own; it loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address.

Questions about any of this? Message us on WhatsApp.
