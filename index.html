Our Story

Tamu Ridge started in 2019 with one idea: the best coffee in the world grows a few hours from our door, yet most Kenyans drink it stale or never taste it at all. We roast on the slopes above Nyeri, in batches small enough to taste every one before it ships.

  • Beans bought directly from smallholder cooperatives in Nyeri and Kirinyaga.
  • Roasted every Monday and Thursday, never stored for weeks.
  • Resealable, farm-labelled bags with the roast date on every one.

Kenyan coffee is famous for its bright, blackcurrant-like acidity and juicy body, thanks to altitude, volcanic soil and careful washed processing. We agree a premium with each cooperative before harvest, share the cupping score of every lot with the farmers who grew it, and print the farm name on every bag. You get coffee with a story you can check and a cup that tastes like where it grew.

Meet the Team

Fresh, traceable and fairly bought: coffee the way it should taste

Every bag we sell can be traced to a cooperative, a harvest and a roast date. These are the three questions customers ask us most.

01 Where exactly does my coffee come from?

From smallholder cooperatives on the slopes of Mt Kenya, mainly in Nyeri and Kirinyaga counties, at 1,600 to 1,900 metres above sea level. Each bag names the cooperative, the harvest year and the grade, for example AA or AB.

02 How fresh is it when it arrives?

We roast on Mondays and Thursdays and pack the next morning. Nairobi orders usually arrive within 48 hours of roasting and the roast date is printed on every bag. We never ship beans older than seven days.

03 Is it fairly traded?

We buy directly, so there is no middleman between you and the cooperative. We agree the price before harvest and share the cupping score of every lot with the farmers who grew it. Ask us what a farmer was paid and we will tell you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery and brewing. Can't find yours? Send us a WhatsApp message.

How do I order?

Message us on WhatsApp with the coffee or plan you want. We confirm the price and delivery date, you pay by M-Pesa (payment details are sent in the chat), and we roast and ship on the next roast day.

Do you deliver outside Nyeri and Nairobi?

Yes. We ship countrywide through courier partners; delivery usually takes 2 to 4 working days and the fee depends on your town. Nyeri and Nairobi delivery is free on the Daily Drinker and Office Pot plans.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean is always best because coffee loses aroma quickly once ground. If you do not own a grinder, tell us how you brew (filter, French press, moka or espresso) and we will grind to match.

How should I store my coffee?

Keep the bag sealed in a cool, dark cupboard away from the stove. Do not refrigerate it. Finish a 250 g bag within four weeks of the roast date for the best flavour.

Can I pause or cancel my plan?

Yes, at any time. Send a message before Wednesday noon and we will skip or stop your next delivery. There are no cancellation fees.
