Fresh-roasted coffee, delivered monthly.

Choose your coffee once and we will roast, pack and deliver it every month, timed so your bag arrives within days of the roast.

  • Three plans, from one 250 g bag to a 2 kg office pot
  • Whole bean or ground to match how you brew
  • Swap coffees, skip a month or cancel by sending one message

Plans start at KES 1,100 a month. Delivery is free in Nyeri and Nairobi on the Daily Drinker and Office Pot plans, and available countrywide by courier at cost.

Every delivery includes a card with the farm story, tasting notes and a simple recipe for that coffee.

Start a subscription

Dependable coffee for teams and customers.

From a five-person office to a busy café, we supply fresh coffee on a schedule that suits you, so you never run out on a Monday morning.

  • 1 kg and 5 kg bags, ground or whole bean
  • Weekly or fortnightly delivery on a fixed day
  • Grinder set-up and a staff brewing session included

Registered businesses receive one monthly invoice, payable by M-Pesa Paybill or bank transfer.

Cafés can request a free tasting of three coffees before committing, and we will help you price your cup so the numbers work.

Request a tasting

Learn to taste and brew with us.

Our Saturday sessions at the Nyeri tasting room are small, relaxed and hands-on, and you leave able to make a better cup at home.

  • Cupping: learn to taste acidity, body and sweetness
  • Pour-over, French press and espresso basics
  • Maximum eight people so everyone gets a turn

Sessions run from 10:00 am to 12:30 pm and cost KES 2,500 per person, including a 250 g bag to take home.

Offices in Nairobi can book a private team session at their premises on request.
